## 1. The headline finding

The blog is **strong on Amazon FBA mechanics and EU customs, but it has not yet planted a flag on the three search waves that are driving the most panic-buying intent in our core markets right now:**

1. **The end of US de minimis (Section 321).** This is the single biggest fulfillment story of 2026. We own a dedicated **EU** de minimis page, but the far larger **US** query is only mentioned in passing inside the tariffs post — it does not own a page. This is our highest-value gap.
2. **TikTok Shop fulfillment.** Zero coverage. A brand-new, fast-growing channel with sellers actively searching how to fulfill from China after TikTok's 2026 logistics changes.
3. **The China + US hybrid model.** The dominant strategic narrative for scaling brands in 2026 ("bestsellers in the US, long-tail from China"). Our content still frames China-vs-US 3PL as either/or; the market has moved to *both*.

These three are warm-to-hot: the searcher has a problem **today** (their landed cost just jumped, their channel rules just changed) and is looking for an operator, not a definition. That is exactly the audience that converts.

## 3. SERP findings — the four forces driving warm traffic now

Live SERP research surfaced four demand waves. Each is a cluster of high-intent queries where a China 3PL is the obvious answer — and where our competitors (Unicargo, ShipBob, SendFromChina, NextSmartShip, Portless) are already publishing and we are mostly absent.

### Wave 1 — "De minimis is gone, what do I do?"
The $800 US exemption was suspended for China in May 2025 and globally in Aug 2025; a Feb 2026 proclamation confirmed no restoration. Every sub-$800 parcel now needs a formal/informal entry, an HTS code, and full duty; postal shipments face $80–$200/item specific duties or ad-valorem IEEPA tariffs. **This breaks the direct-small-parcel model and pushes sellers toward consolidation + DDP + bulk import — i.e. exactly our services.** Query intent here is red-hot.

### Wave 2 — "The channel rules just changed" (TikTok Shop, Temu, Shein)
- **TikTok Shop** is phasing out independent shipping for US *local* sellers (Feb–Mar 2026), forcing FBT/Upgraded Shipping. But **cross-border (CBT) sellers shipping from China are a growing segment we can serve** — and almost nobody is writing the China-side playbook.
- **Temu/Shein semi-managed** mode lets sellers fulfill from their *own* overseas/China inventory rather than the platform's full-managed pipeline. These sellers need a fulfillment partner — and don't know who to use.

### Wave 3 — "Tariffs broke my unit economics; where do I put my inventory?"
The strongest emerging framework is the **China + US hybrid model**: bestsellers/high-velocity SKUs in a US 3PL for 2–4 day delivery; long-tail, new-SKU tests, and non-US markets fulfilled direct from China. We are the China leg of that model — but our content tells sellers to pick *one*. Reframing to "split your inventory" captures the actual decision sellers are making.

### Wave 4 — "I need the China-side mechanics explained" (DDP, IOR, landed cost, returns)
High-volume informational queries — "DDP shipping from China meaning," "who pays the duty," "how do returns work when I ship from China" — that are semi-warm, own our core differentiator (DDP/zero-surprise-duty), and feed AEO/AI-Overview citations. We mention DDP on every page but don't own the explainer query.

## 5. The 12 recommended posts (prioritised briefs)

> Format note: every post should follow the existing strong template — specific numbers with units, a comparison table, an FAQ block with `FAQPage` schema, a visible "Last updated" date, and a "Related services" internal-link block. That pattern is already working; keep it.

### TIER 1 — Publish first (hot intent, biggest gaps)

**1. "US De Minimis Is Gone: The 2026 Survival Guide for Sellers Shipping from China"**
- **Target:** `us de minimis ended 2026` / `section 321 de minimis 2026` / `shipping from china to us after de minimis`
- **Audience:** Amazon + Shopify + crowdfunding (US). **Geo: US.**
- **Why now:** Our single biggest gap. We already proved this format works with the EU de minimis page — clone the playbook for the far bigger US query. Every direct-small-parcel seller's model just broke; they are searching this *today*.
- **Angle:** What changed and when → the new per-parcel cost math (formal entry + IEEPA + $80–$200/item postal) → why consolidation + DDP + bulk import is the fix → our $0.49/CBM + DDP offer.
- **Internal links:** tariff-management, china-consolidation, amazon-fba-consolidation, the EU de minimis post (sibling).

**2. "TikTok Shop Fulfillment from China: The 2026 Cross-Border Seller's Playbook"**
- **Target:** `tiktok shop fulfillment from china` / `tiktok shop cross-border seller` / `fulfill tiktok shop orders from china`
- **Audience:** TikTok Shop / D2C sellers. **Geo: US, UK.**
- **Why now:** Brand-new channel, zero coverage, and TikTok's Feb–Mar 2026 logistics shake-up has sellers scrambling. Clarify the FBT vs CBT split and own the China-side cross-border angle that the big US 3PLs ignore.
- **Angle:** What TikTok's 2026 changes mean for sellers sourcing in China → how cross-border (CBT) fulfillment from Shenzhen works → speed/cost vs holding US stock → handling viral-spike replenishment.
- **Internal links:** ecommerce-fulfillment, express-international-shipping, dropshipping-fulfillment.

**3. "Bestsellers in the US, Long-Tail from China: The Hybrid Fulfillment Model for 2026"**
- **Target:** `china us hybrid fulfillment` / `split inventory china us warehouse` / `where to store inventory tariffs 2026`
- **Audience:** Scaling Shopify + Amazon brands. **Geo: US.**
- **Why now:** This is *the* 2026 framework and it positions us perfectly as the China leg. Reframes our dated "China vs US 3PL" either/or content into the "run both" decision sellers are actually making.
- **Angle:** The split logic (velocity/predictability) → the tariff math for each leg → how to test new SKUs in China before committing US stock → our role in the model.
- **Internal links:** direct-from-china-d2c-vs-us-3pl-2026 (update that post to point here), china-consolidation, ecommerce-fulfillment.

**4. "DDP Shipping from China, Explained: Who Actually Pays the Duty in 2026"**
- **Target:** `ddp shipping from china meaning` / `what is ddp shipping` / `who pays duty ddp` / `importer of record china`
- **Audience:** All. **Geo: global.** High-volume informational → semi-warm.
- **Why now:** Owns our core differentiator (zero-surprise-duty), strong AEO/AI-Overview citation potential, and feeds every commercial page. A definition/glossary piece that ranks broadly and funnels down.
- **Angle:** DDP vs DAP plain-English → who is the importer of record → why DDP protects D2C conversion → the post-de-minimis reason it now matters more.
- **Internal links:** every shipping + consolidation page; make this the canonical "what is DDP" hub.
