## Production notes
- All 6 pages are `noindex`, out of the sitemap, and have no blog card (staged, not published). Generating the images completes them.
- The 6 infographics are embedded in-body, so they must exist at the paths above before publish or the in-body `<img>` will 404.
- **Rebuild caution:** running `scripts/build_blog.py` regenerates every post's HTML from its draft and re-adds every blog card — which strips the `noindex` lines from held posts and un-hides them. After any rebuild, re-stage held posts (add `noindex`, remove blog cards) before committing. Live posts also carry hand-added `hreflang`/`author` meta the build does not reproduce, so avoid rebuilding them.
- **To publish later (per post):** delete the `noindex` line (tagged `UNPUBLISHED: remove this line to publish`), add the sitemap entry (dates Oct 1 / 6 / 13 / 15 / 20 / 23), add the blog card, then merge to `main`.
