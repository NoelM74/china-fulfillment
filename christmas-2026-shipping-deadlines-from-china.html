Christmas 2026 Shipping Deadlines from China: The Last Dispatch Dates for D2C Orders

By Noel Murphy Published October 24, 2026

Quick answer: For delivery before Christmas Day, Friday 25 December 2026, dispatch standard D2C orders from Shenzhen by Friday 4 December for the EU, Wednesday 9 December for the US, Thursday 10 December for Canada, and Friday 11 December for the UK and Australia. Express courier orders can go until Tuesday 15 December. Each date is the slow end of our published delivery time plus a buffer for peak-season volumes. Set your store's cut-off a day or two earlier than the dispatch date, and switch to express-only after it.

Christmas is the one week of the year when "6 to 10 days" is not good enough as an answer. Your customer wants to know whether the gift arrives before the 25th, and you want to know the last day you can safely say yes.

This guide gives the dates for each main market, explains why they sit earlier than the normal transit time, and covers the November calendar that comes first. It is written for brands shipping orders direct to consumers from a warehouse in China.

The last dispatch dates for Christmas 2026

These are the dates by which an order should leave our Shenzhen warehouse to reach the customer by Thursday 24 December.

Market Our published delivery time Recommended last dispatch
European Union 6–14 days Friday 4 December
United States 6–10 days Wednesday 9 December
Canada 5–9 days Thursday 10 December
United Kingdom 5–8 days Friday 11 December
Australia 4–7 days Friday 11 December
Express upgrade, any of the above 2–5 business days Tuesday 15 December

How we set them. Each standard date takes the slow end of our published delivery range and adds five days for peak-season volumes. The express date adds two business days to the slowest express time. Where the result fell on a weekend, we moved it to the Friday before.

Your store's cut-off should be earlier. An order placed at 11 pm on 9 December is not dispatched on 9 December. Put your "order by" date a day or two ahead of the dispatch date, and confirm our daily dispatch cut-off time with your account manager.

Why the cut-offs come earlier than the transit time

Carriers are at full stretch. DHL Express applies a demand surcharge to its international time-definite and day-definite services from 1 October 2026 to 5 February 2027. FedEx's 2026 peak pricing in the US runs until 17 January and is highest from 23 November to 27 December. Surcharges are the visible part. The invisible part is that hubs and delivery routes run full, and a parcel that misses one connection waits for the next.

Every US parcel now clears customs individually. The US de minimis exemption is still suspended, so each parcel from China needs a customs entry and pays duty. Parcels with vague descriptions, missing HTS codes or values that do not match the invoice get held. In December, held means late. Our guide to shipping D2C orders from China to the US in 2027 explains what each parcel pays and how it is collected.

The last mile peaks too. Even a parcel that clears on time joins the destination country's own Christmas surge for the final delivery.

The November calendar comes first

Date What happens What it means for you
Wed 11 November Singles' Day, China's biggest shopping day Domestic parcel networks in China run at their peak; book carrier capacity and pickups early
Thu 26 November US Thanksgiving US carriers run reduced services
Fri 27 November Black Friday Your order volume jumps; make sure stock is already in our warehouse
Mon 30 November Cyber Monday The second spike, and the start of the run to the Christmas cut-offs
Fri 4 to Fri 11 December Standard cut-offs by market Switch each market to express-only as its date passes
Tue 15 December Express cut-off After this, sell gift cards or January delivery

Stock has to be in place before Black Friday. Goods arriving at our warehouse during the peak still get received, but they are competing with everyone else's peak stock for the same docks. Aim to have Q4 inventory received, counted and ready to pick by mid-November. Receiving is free.

Market by market

United States. Ship DDP so the duty is paid before the parcel arrives and your customer is never asked for money at the door. A gift that arrives with a duty bill attached is the fastest way to a refund request. Our DDP explainer covers how it works.

United Kingdom. For orders of £135 or less, UK VAT is charged at checkout, so the parcel itself carries no doorstep bill. Our UK fulfilment guide covers the rule.

European Union. Since 1 July 2026 the EU charges a flat €3 per item category on low-value parcels, alongside VAT collected through IOSS. Parcels with missing or wrong IOSS data are the ones held at the border. Our EU de minimis guide explains it.

Canada. The de minimis for goods from China is only CAD$20, so most gifts are dutiable and taxable. Ship DDP. See our Canada guide.

Australia. For orders of A$1,000 or less, GST is collected at checkout by a GST-registered seller or marketplace. See our Australia guide.

After the standard cut-off: sell the upgrade

When a market's standard date passes, do not simply stop taking Christmas orders. Switch that market to an express-only Christmas option at checkout, priced to cover the courier rate. Express couriers from Shenzhen deliver in 2 to 5 business days, so the 15 December dispatch date still leaves a margin before Christmas Eve.

After 15 December, the honest offer is a gift card or a clearly labelled "arrives after Christmas" option. Customers forgive "it will arrive on the 29th" when you tell them up front. They do not forgive silence.

Your Christmas checklist

  1. Put a cut-off banner on your store for each market, a day or two before our dispatch date.
  2. Add the express-only Christmas option for after the standard cut-off.
  3. Check that every SKU has a clean description, HTS code and customs value, so parcels do not stall at customs.
  4. Confirm DDP is on for the US, Canada and the EU.
  5. Plan gift extras now. Gift notes, inserts and bundles need to be set up before the peak. Our kitting starts from $0.50 per unit, and packing in your own branded materials carries no surcharge.
  6. Publish your Christmas returns window, and read our guide to handling returns when you fulfil from China before January.
  7. Order January stock now. Chinese New Year falls on 6 February 2027, and our CNY 2027 deadlines guide has the production cut-offs.

Does China close for Christmas?

No. Christmas is not a public holiday in mainland China, so our Shenzhen warehouse and the carriers keep working through late December. The holidays that matter are New Year's Day on 1 January and Chinese New Year on 6 February 2027, which disrupts factories and freight for several weeks. For a D2C brand fulfilling from China, December is a normal working month at origin. The pressure is all at the destination end.

The bottom line

Christmas 2026 falls on a Friday, and the realistic last dispatch dates from China run from 4 December for the EU to 11 December for the UK and Australia, with express until 15 December. Put your store cut-offs a day or two earlier, have peak stock in place before Black Friday, ship DDP with clean customs data, and switch to express-only once each standard date has passed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last day to ship from China for Christmas 2026?

For delivery by 24 December 2026, we recommend dispatching standard D2C orders from Shenzhen by Friday 4 December for the EU, Wednesday 9 December for the US, Thursday 10 December for Canada, and Friday 11 December for the UK and Australia. Express courier orders can go until Tuesday 15 December. Each date is the slow end of our published delivery time plus a peak-season buffer.

How long does shipping from China to the US take in December?

Our standard D2C delivery time from Shenzhen to US customers is 6 to 10 days, and express couriers take 2 to 5 business days. In December, carrier and customs volumes are at their highest, so plan to the slow end of each range and add a buffer of several days rather than relying on the fast end.

Do carriers charge extra to ship during the holiday season?

Yes. DHL Express applies a demand surcharge to international time-definite and day-definite services from 1 October 2026 to 5 February 2027. FedEx's 2026 peak pricing in the US runs until 17 January 2027 and is highest from 23 November to 27 December. These surcharges are added on top of normal rates and fuel surcharges.

Will US customs delay my Christmas parcels from China?

Every parcel from China now needs a customs entry and pays duty, because the US de minimis exemption is still suspended. Parcels shipped DDP with accurate descriptions, HTS codes and values clear fastest. CBP has said there are no changes to international mail entry procedures before 20 January 2027, so the new postal entry type does not affect Christmas deliveries.

Does China close for Christmas?

No. Christmas is not a public holiday in mainland China, so Shenzhen warehouses and carriers keep working through late December. The next breaks are New Year's Day on 1 January and Chinese New Year, which falls on 6 February 2027 and affects production and freight for several weeks.

Should I offer express shipping for late Christmas orders?

Yes. Once the standard cut-off for a market has passed, switch your store to an express-only option for Christmas delivery and charge for it. Express couriers from Shenzhen deliver in 2 to 5 business days, so dispatching by 15 December leaves a buffer before Christmas Eve. After that, promote gift cards or post-Christmas delivery instead.
