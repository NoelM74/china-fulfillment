# How Much Does China Fulfilment Cost? The 2026 Pricing Guide

![A cost breakdown of China fulfilment in 2026 covering pick and pack, storage, prep and shipping, mapped against a Shenzhen warehouse operation](/images/blog/china-fulfillment-cost-pricing-2026.webp)

"How much does it cost?" is the question every seller actually wants answered, and the one most fulfilment providers bury behind a contact form. So here is a straight answer, with real numbers. China fulfilment is priced in a handful of lines: pick and pack, storage, any prep or value-added work, and shipping. The handling side is genuinely cheaper at the source than in the West; the shipping side is the big variable and depends on your product and destination. This guide breaks down each line, shows what we charge, and flags the hidden costs that make a cheap headline rate expensive in practice.

> **Quick answer:** China fulfilment handling is low-cost: our pick and pack is $0.99 per order with no minimums, storage is $0.49 per CBM per day (first 30 days free), and there are zero receiving fees. Prep is priced per unit (labelling from $0.15, poly bagging from $0.30, kitting from $0.50), and standard visual inspection is free. Shipping is the biggest variable and is quoted by weight and destination. For comparison, Western 3PLs often charge around $2.75 just for the first item's pick and pack.

## The lines that make up a China fulfilment quote

Every fulfilment quote, wherever in the world, is built from the same components. Here is what each one is and what we charge.

| Cost line | What it covers | Our published rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Pick and pack** | Retrieving items and packing each order | **$0.99 per order**, no minimums |
| **Storage** | Holding inventory, charged by volume | **$0.49 per CBM / day**, first 30 days free |
| **Receiving** | Booking your stock into the warehouse | **Zero** receiving fees |
| **Labelling** | FNSKU / barcode labelling for FBA and marketplaces | from **$0.15 per unit** |
| **Poly bagging** | Protective bagging where required | from **$0.30 per unit** |
| **Kitting / bundling** | Assembling sets, bundles and multipacks | from **$0.50 per unit** |
| **Inspection** | Standard visual quality check | **Free** |
| **Shipping** | Carriage to the customer or destination | Quoted by weight and destination |

The handling lines are fixed and transparent. The one that moves is shipping, and it is worth being honest about why.

## Why shipping is quoted, not fixed

Nobody can give you a single, honest "shipping from China costs $X" number, and you should be wary of anyone who tries. Shipping depends on the **weight and dimensions of the item and the destination country**, and the mode you choose:

- **Express courier** (DHL, FedEx, UPS) for fast direct-to-consumer parcels. We pass on carrier discounts of up to 40% on standard rates.
- **Air freight** for faster bulk movements, from roughly $4.50 to $8.50 per kg depending on lane and service.
- **Sea freight** for bulk inventory, with less-than-container-load from around $300 per CBM and full containers from approximately $1,800 for a 20-foot container to the US.

A light, small item ships cheaply by express; a heavy or bulky one may only make economic sense by sea. This is why a real quote beats a headline number: the same $0.99 pick and pack sits in front of wildly different shipping costs depending on what you sell and where it goes. Our [express and freight options](/express-international-shipping.html) are quoted per shipment for exactly this reason.

## How China compares with a Western 3PL

On **handling and storage, China is generally cheaper**, and the reason is simple: labour and warehouse space cost less at the source. Western 3PLs commonly charge around **$2.75 for the first item** in pick and pack alone, before storage and prep, against our $0.99 per order. Prep work, labelling, bagging, kitting, follows the same pattern, which is a large part of why sellers [prep for Amazon FBA in China](/amazon-fba-prep-china.html) rather than paying destination rates.

**Shipping is where it gets nuanced.** Individual parcels from China can cost more per unit than domestic last-mile delivery inside the destination country. That is why the cost-optimal answer for a growing brand is usually not "all China" or "all destination", but a mix: import your proven sellers in bulk and fulfil them locally, and ship the long tail direct from China. We work through that maths in detail in our guide to [shipping direct to FBA versus staging first](/amazon-fba-direct-vs-us-3pl-first-2026.html).

## The hidden costs that make a cheap rate expensive

A low pick-and-pack headline can hide the lines that actually cost you money. When you compare providers, ask about these:

- **Receiving fees.** Some charge to book your stock in. Ours are zero.
- **Storage after any free period, and long-term penalties.** A free first month means little if the ongoing rate is high or penalties bite. Ours is $0.49 per CBM per day with no long-term penalties.
- **Minimum-order charges.** Minimums quietly raise your effective per-order cost at low volume. We have none.
- **Duty.** This is the big one for 2026. With [US de minimis ended](/us-de-minimis-ended-2026-china-sellers-guide.html) and the EU's low-value rules changed, parcels that used to clear duty-free now owe duty. It has to be priced in, and ideally prepaid through [DDP](/ddp-shipping-from-china-explained-2026.html) so the customer is never billed at the door and you are never surprised by it.

Add those up and the "cheapest" quote is often not the cheapest once the real cost lands.

## A worked way to think about it

For a light e-commerce product shipped direct to a customer, your per-order cost is roughly: **$0.99 pick and pack + any prep + the express shipping quote + prepaid duty.** Storage is a small monthly line spread across your inventory, near zero in the first 30 days. For bulk movements into a destination warehouse or Amazon, the model flips to a low per-unit sea-freight cost plus one bulk customs entry, then domestic fulfilment at the other end. Which is cheaper per order depends on your volume, weight and where your customers are, and that is precisely the calculation a quote answers and a blog post cannot.

## The bottom line

China fulfilment is transparently cheap on the parts you control, $0.99 pick and pack, $0.49 per CBM per day storage with 30 days free, free inspection, zero receiving fees, and priced honestly on the part you cannot fix, shipping, which depends on your product and destination. The real skill is putting duty, prep and the bulk-versus-direct mix into one number that reflects your actual business.
