# China-to-US Fulfilment for Beauty & Skincare Brands (2026): MoCRA, Unboxing and Batch Control

![Beauty and skincare products being kitted and packed with branded unboxing in a Shenzhen warehouse for DDP shipping to US customers in 2026](/images/blog/china-to-us-fulfilment-beauty-skincare-brands.webp)

Beauty and skincare is one of the biggest Shopify categories sourcing from China, and one of the least forgiving to fulfil badly. The products are regulated, many have a shelf life, the unboxing is part of the brand, and US customs now wants duty on every parcel. Get those four things right and a China-based beauty brand can deliver a genuinely premium experience straight to US doorsteps. Get any of them wrong and you are looking at border detentions, expired stock, or a flat grey mailer that undoes your whole brand.

This guide separates what you must handle before shipping from what a fulfilment partner handles for you, and shows how beauty fulfilment actually runs from Shenzhen.

> **Quick answer:** You own the regulatory side, MoCRA facility registration, US agent, product listing and labelling, before anything ships. Your fulfilment partner owns the logistics: temperature-sensible storage, batch and expiry control, branded unboxing, and DDP shipping so US customers pay no duty at the door. After de minimis, every cosmetic parcel from China owes duty, so DDP is essential.

## First, the part that is on you: MoCRA

Before we talk fulfilment, the compliance reality, because no amount of good logistics rescues a product that gets stopped at the border.

US cosmetics are regulated under the Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act, MoCRA. In broad terms, both domestic and foreign facilities that manufacture or process cosmetics for the US market must register with the FDA, and a foreign facility must designate a US agent who is physically present in the United States. Registrations renew every two years, and each cosmetic product must be listed with the FDA with its ingredients and manufacturing information. The enforcement edge is simple: products from unregistered facilities, or containing prohibited ingredients, can be detained and refused at the border.

Here is the important line for this article: **this is the brand's responsibility, not the fulfilment warehouse's.** Facility registration, the US agent, product listing, safety substantiation and compliant labelling are yours to put in place with your manufacturer and a regulatory specialist before goods ship. A 3PL does not register your facility or file your listings. What a good one does is ship your already-compliant product correctly, and flag when something looks off before it becomes a border problem. Treat any provider who claims to "handle FDA compliance for you" with caution.

## What fulfilment actually handles for a beauty brand

With compliance in place, the logistics are where a beauty brand is won or lost day to day.

**Batch and expiry control.** Cosmetics need lot tracking and, for anything with a shelf life, first-expiry-first-out picking so older stock always ships first. We record the batch against each shipment and rotate by expiry, which supports the traceability a brand must keep for recalls or adverse-event reporting and stops you writing off stock that aged out on the shelf. Our [quality-control and inspection process](/quality-control-fba-prep-china.html) checks condition before anything is put away.

**Sensible storage.** Skincare does not enjoy sitting in a hot warehouse. Stock should be held in conditions appropriate to the product and moved before it ages, which is a function of both storage and stock rotation.

**The unboxing.** This is where beauty fulfilment earns its keep. Beauty buyers photograph and film what arrives, so [branded packaging, tissue, inserts and sample kitting](/branded-packaging-kitting.html) are part of the product, not an extra. Doing that [kitting at the source in Shenzhen](/source-side-kitting-bundle-assembly-china.html), assembling gift sets, adding a free sample, building a subscription box, costs a fraction of what it costs downstream, and it is what turns a first order into a repeat one. Runs start at 50 pieces, so you can test a bundle without a big commitment.

## After de minimis, cosmetics owe duty too

A beauty brand costing its US orders on the old duty-free assumption is in for a shock. With US [de minimis suspended](/us-de-minimis-ended-2026-china-sellers-guide.html), cosmetics shipped from China owe duty on arrival whatever the parcel is worth, on top of the MoCRA requirements. So every direct-to-consumer parcel should go [DDP](/ddp-shipping-from-china-explained-2026.html), with the duty prepaid, so your customer is never billed at the door, an especially bad experience for a brand selling a treat-yourself product. As volume grows, importing in bulk and fulfilling from the US, or drip-feeding, lowers the per-unit duty and handling.

## A sensible setup for a China-based beauty brand

- **Get compliant first.** MoCRA registration, US agent, product listing and labelling sorted with your manufacturer and adviser before a single unit ships.
- **Fulfil direct from Shenzhen** to start: [DDP express](/express-international-shipping.html) to US customers, batch-tracked, with branded unboxing built in.
- **Kit and bundle at the source** to lift average order value and make the unboxing content-worthy.
- **Graduate winners into US stock** once volume justifies it, so bestsellers get fast domestic delivery while the rest stays light in China.
