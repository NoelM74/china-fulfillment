# Valentine's Day 2027 Shipping Deadlines from China: Beat the Chinese New Year Close

![A red gift box tied with a red satin ribbon on a white surface, the kind of Valentine's order that has to leave China before the Chinese New Year close](/images/blog/valentines-day-2027-shipping-deadlines-from-china.webp)

> **Quick answer:** Valentine's Day 2027 is **Sunday 14 February**, eight days after **Chinese New Year on Saturday 6 February**. Our Shenzhen warehouse is **closed from 6 to 14 February**, so the last day any order leaves is **Friday 5 February**. For standard delivery by Friday 12 February, dispatch by **Friday 29 January for the EU**, **Saturday 30 January for Canada and Australia**, **Tuesday 2 February for the US** and **Thursday 4 February for the UK**. Express orders can go until **Friday 5 February**.

Most years, Valentine's Day is an easy peak for brands shipping from China. In 2027 it is not. Chinese New Year falls on 6 February, and Valentine's Day lands eight days later, while China is on holiday.

That moves every Valentine's deadline earlier than usual. This post gives the dates for each main market, explains how they were set, and covers the stock and store changes to make before the close.

## The last dispatch dates for Valentine's Day 2027

These are the dates by which an order should **leave our Shenzhen warehouse** to reach the customer by Friday 12 February, ahead of the weekend.

| Market | Standard delivery time | Valentine's dispatch cut-off |
|---|---|---|
| European Union | 6–14 days | **Friday 29 January** |
| Canada | 9–12 days | **Saturday 30 January** |
| Australia | 8–12 days | **Saturday 30 January** |
| United States | 6–10 days | **Tuesday 2 February** |
| United Kingdom | 5–8 days | **Thursday 4 February** |
| Express upgrade, any of the above | 2–5 business days | **Friday 5 February** |

**How the dates were set.** Each date is the slowest end of our standard delivery time for that market, counted back from Friday 12 February. Most parcels arrive sooner. But a cut-off that only works when the parcel is fast is not a date you can promise a customer.

**Shipping somewhere else, or on a different service?** The table covers our main D2C markets. We ship to more than 200 countries with over 50 carriers, each with its own timing. [Contact us](Contact-Us.html) for the exact cut-off for your countries and services.

**Your store's cut-off should be earlier.** An order placed late on 2 February is not dispatched on 2 February. Put your "order by" date a day or two ahead of the dispatch date.

![Valentine's Day 2027 dispatch cut-offs from Shenzhen for delivery by 12 February: EU Friday 29 January, Canada and Australia Saturday 30 January, US Tuesday 2 February, UK Thursday 4 February, express Friday 5 February; warehouse closed 6 to 14 February for Chinese New Year](/images/blog/infographic-valentines-2027-dispatch-dates.webp)

## Our Chinese New Year closure

Our warehouse is **closed from Saturday 6 February to Sunday 14 February 2027** and reopens on **Monday 15 February**.

- **Friday 5 February is the last dispatch day** before the holiday, for every market and every service.
- **Orders placed from 6 to 14 February** are dispatched from Monday 15 February. They will not arrive for Valentine's Day.
- **Expect a backlog on reopening.** Orders that built up over the holiday all ship in the first days back, so allow a little extra time for those.

## Why Valentine's 2027 is harder than usual

**The holiday is right before the peak.** In a normal year, the run-up to Valentine's Day is ordinary working time at origin. In 2027 the last week before 14 February is the Chinese New Year holiday, so everything has to happen earlier.

**Factories close before the holiday, not on it.** Production winds down in the weeks before Chinese New Year, as workers travel home. Our [Chinese New Year 2027 guide](chinese-new-year-2027-shipping-deadlines.html) covers the production and freight cut-offs.

**Carriers are still in peak season.** DHL Express applies a demand surcharge to its international services from 1 October 2026 to 5 February 2027, and capacity out of China is tight in the run-up to the holiday.

**Every US parcel clears customs individually.** The US de minimis exemption is still suspended, so each parcel from China needs a customs entry and pays duty. Parcels with vague descriptions or wrong values get held, and before Valentine's Day, held means late. Our guide to [shipping D2C orders to the US in 2027](shipping-d2c-orders-china-to-us-2027.html) covers what each parcel pays.

## What to change on your store

1. **Show a Valentine's cut-off banner** for each market, a day or two before our dispatch date.
2. **Offer an express-only Valentine's option** after the standard date passes, priced to cover the courier rate. Express from Shenzhen takes 2 to 5 business days, so Friday 5 February is the last day it still works.
3. **From 6 February, say "ships from 15 February"** on product pages and at checkout. A gift card or a "late Valentine's" offer is the honest alternative.
4. **Check every SKU's customs data**: a clear description, HTS code and value, so parcels do not stall at the border.
5. **Ship DDP** to the US, Canada and the EU, so a gift never arrives with a duty bill attached.
6. **Plan gift extras now.** Gift notes, inserts and bundles need setting up before January. Kitting starts from $0.50 per unit, and packing in your own branded materials carries no surcharge.

## Market notes

**United States.** Duty applies to every parcel. Ship DDP and price the duty in.

**United Kingdom.** For orders of £135 or less, UK VAT is charged at checkout, so the parcel carries no doorstep bill. See our [UK fulfilment guide](china-to-uk-fulfilment-shopify.html).

**European Union.** Since 1 July 2026 the EU charges a flat €3 per item category on low-value parcels, alongside VAT collected through IOSS. See our [EU de minimis guide](eu-de-minimis-2026-customs-duty-guide.html).

**Canada.** Goods from China are duty and tax free only up to CAD$20, so most gifts are dutiable and taxable. Ship DDP. See our [Canada guide](china-to-canada-fulfillment-2026.html).

**Australia.** For orders of A$1,000 or less, GST is collected at checkout by a GST-registered seller or marketplace. See our [Australia guide](china-to-australia-fulfillment-2026.html).

In 2027, Valentine's Day falls during the Chinese New Year holiday, so the real deadline is not 14 February but the day each order leaves China. Get stock in by mid-January, ship standard orders by the dates above, switch to express until Friday 5 February, and tell customers clearly what happens after that.
