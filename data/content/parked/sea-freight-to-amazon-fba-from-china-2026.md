# Sea Freight to Amazon FBA from China: The Steps, the Real Timeline and the Landed Cost

![Aerial view of a loaded container ship moored alongside a quay under three ship-to-shore cranes at a busy container terminal](/images/blog/sea-freight-to-amazon-fba-from-china-2026.webp)

> **Quick answer:** Sea freight is the cheapest way to restock Amazon from China for anything that can wait five weeks or more. From South China, cargo takes about **15–25 days port to port** to the US West Coast and **about 25–35 days from sailing to an Amazon warehouse**, longer to the East Coast and **30–45+ days to Northern Europe**. The ocean rate is one line of eight: the landed cost adds origin handling, the customs entry, duty (now including the **12.5% Section 301 forced labor duty** on China-origin goods), destination charges, delivery to Amazon on a booked appointment and any placement fee. For stock you need selling through **Chinese New Year 2027 on 6 February, sail in December.**

Most sea freight problems with Amazon do not happen at sea. They happen in the week before the cargo sails, when the shipment plan, the labels and the cartons are either right or wrong, and in the week after it lands, when the container has to be cleared, split and booked into a warehouse that sets its own delivery times.

This guide covers the whole run from a China warehouse to an Amazon shelf: the steps in the order they have to happen, the dates that matter this winter, and the cost lines a proper quote should show you. For this month's market rates by lane, see our [FBA freight rates page](fba-freight-from-china.html). This article is about the process behind them.

## The nine steps from a China warehouse to an Amazon shelf

1. **Choose the mode and size the shipment.** Sea suits planned restocks. Air suits launches and stock-outs. Within sea, a shared container (LCL) usually suits about 1 to 15 CBM and a full container (FCL) wins above that, but the line moves with rates. Our [LCL vs FCL guide](lcl-vs-fcl-from-china-2026.html) shows how to find yours.
2. **Create the shipment in Send to Amazon before the cargo leaves.** Amazon assigns the fulfilment centres and generates the box labels. If you accept its split, your cartons may go to several sites. Asking for fewer destinations can carry an inbound placement fee, which our [placement fee guide](amazon-fba-inbound-placement-fees-2026.html) breaks down. Build the plan first and the freight around it, never the other way round.
3. **Prep, label and carton at origin.** Amazon stopped offering prep and item labelling for US inbound shipments from **1 January 2026**, a change it announced on 28 July 2025. FNSKU labels, poly bags, bubble wrap and bundles now have to be done before the goods arrive. Amazon's Seller Central rules cap a box holding several items at **25 inches on any side and 50 lb**, and a pallet at **40 × 48 inches, 72 inches high and 1,500 lb**. Check the current page before you build to it, because Amazon revises these.
4. **Hand over and export from China.** Full containers are loaded at our dock or the factory and trucked to the terminal. LCL cargo goes to a consolidation warehouse, called a container freight station or CFS, by its cut-off, which is usually about five days before the sailing. Export customs is cleared in China.
5. **File the ISF before loading.** For ocean cargo to the US, the Importer Security Filing, known as "10+2", must reach CBP at least **24 hours before the cargo is loaded** at the foreign port. A late or missing filing can draw liquidated damages of up to $5,000.
6. **The ocean leg.** About 15 to 25 days port to port to Los Angeles or Long Beach. New York is longer, via the Panama Canal or Suez.
7. **Customs entry and duty.** The importer of record files the entry and pays the duty and fees, backed by a customs bond. Amazon will not act as your importer, and neither does a 3PL just by shipping your goods. Our [importer of record guide](importer-of-record-china-fulfillment-2026.html) explains who it is under each arrangement.
8. **Port to Amazon.** A full container is trucked from the port, either straight to the fulfilment centre or to a cross-dock to be split across Amazon's destinations. LCL cargo is unloaded at a destination CFS first. Either way, delivery into Amazon needs an appointment. With your own carrier it is requested through Amazon's Carrier Appointment Request Portal, and Amazon says the site responds within 24 hours with a time. With an Amazon-partnered carrier, Amazon books it for you.
9. **Check-in at Amazon.** Stock is only sellable once the fulfilment centre has received and scanned it. Check-in time varies by site and season, and it is slowest in Q4 and in the weeks after Chinese New Year.

## The real timeline, and the dates to work back from this winter

Port-to-port transit is the number forwarders quote, and it is the one that misleads. What matters is the date your stock can be sold.

| Stage | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Production finished and delivered to the warehouse | Set by your factory |
| Inspection, prep, labelling and pallets | Before the sailing date |
| South China port to US West Coast port | About 15–25 days |
| Sailing to a US West Coast Amazon warehouse | About 25–35 days |
| South China to Northern Europe by sea | About 30–45+ days, mostly via the Cape |
| Amazon check-in | Varies by site and season |

**Chinese New Year 2027 falls on 6 February.** Our [CNY 2027 deadlines guide](chinese-new-year-2027-shipping-deadlines.html) sets the cut-offs: confirm production by early December 2026, and book and hand over sea freight by mid-January 2027.

Put the transit times next to those dates and the answer for Amazon sellers is blunt. Cargo handed over at the mid-January cut-off reaches a West Coast Amazon warehouse at roughly **9 to 19 February**, before check-in, by which time the holiday has started and your competitors are already out of stock. Mid-January is the last sensible window for sea freight at all. **Stock you need selling through February has to sail in December.**

Europe is tighter still. At 30 to 45 days or more by sea, a mid-January sailing lands in late February at best. If Amazon EU stock has to arrive before March, rail is often the only option that fits, and our guide to [shipping from China to Europe](shipping-china-to-europe-sea-rail-truck-air-2026.html) compares the four modes.

### Three things to watch before you book

- **Rates.** Drewry's World Container Index on 22 October 2026 put Shanghai to Los Angeles at **$8,007 per 40ft**, up 2% on the week, and Shanghai to New York at $10,166. The composite was its highest in 15 weeks. Drewry expected transpacific rates to hold steady the following week, but space usually tightens and rates climb in the final weeks before Chinese New Year as everyone ships at once.
- **US port fees on China-linked ships.** The suspension of USTR's Section 301 fees on Chinese-operated and Chinese-built vessels was due to lapse at the end of 9 November 2026. On 23 October USTR announced an extension to 9 January 2027, in a notice due in the Federal Register on 26 October. If the fees return on 10 January, carriers may pass them on during exactly the weeks everyone is shipping.
- **The US–China truce.** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on 23 September that the truce agreed in Busan would run to 10 January 2027. Chinese officials had not confirmed the extension at the time. Either way, it ends in the same pre-holiday window.

## What sea freight to Amazon really costs

The ocean rate is the number everyone compares, and it is one of eight lines. On the same week, forwarders were quoting **$60–120 per CBM** base rate for shared-container cargo to the US West Coast (early October 2026), before port and handling charges. A door-to-Amazon quote should show every line below. If it shows one number, ask what it leaves out.

| Cost line | What it covers | How it is charged |
|---|---|---|
| Prep at origin | FNSKU labels, bagging, cartons built to Amazon's limits | Per unit and per carton |
| Origin charges | Trucking to port or CFS, export declaration, terminal handling | Per shipment or per container |
| Ocean freight and surcharges | The sea leg, fuel and seasonal surcharges | Per container (FCL) or per CBM (LCL) |
| ISF and customs entry | Security filing, brokerage, customs bond | Per shipment; bond per entry or per year |
| Duty and fees | MFN duty, Section 301, the 12.5% forced labor duty, MPF, HMF | Percentage of customs value |
| Destination charges | Terminal handling, CFS unloading for LCL | Per shipment or per CBM |
| Delivery to Amazon | Port drayage, cross-dock, LTL or FTL on appointment | Per container, pallet or truck |
| Amazon placement fee | Charged if you choose fewer inbound destinations | Per unit |

On the first line, our published prices are **$0.99 per carton** for FBA prep pick and pack, FNSKU labelling from **$0.15 per unit** and poly bagging from **$0.30 per unit**, with receiving free. The full list is on our [pricing page](our-pricing.html).

### Duty is the line that grew

Since 24 July 2026, China-origin goods pay a 12.5% Section 301 forced labor duty on top of everything that applied before. Here is how the stack works on an example shipment with a customs value of $20,000, for a product with a 3% MFN duty rate that also sits on Section 301 List 3 at 25%:

| Line | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| MFN duty | 3% | $600.00 |
| Section 301, List 3 | 25% | $5,000.00 |
| Section 301 forced labor duty | 12.5% | $2,500.00 |
| Merchandise processing fee | 0.3464% | $69.28 |
| Harbor maintenance fee (ocean only) | 0.125% | $25.00 |
| **Total** | | **$8,194.28** |

That is about **41% of the goods' value** before a dollar of freight. The merchandise processing fee also has a minimum and a maximum per entry, which CBP updates each October. Your own rates depend on the HTS code, and our [Section 301 forced labor guide](section-301-forced-labor-tariff-china-2026.html) explains what stacks and what is exempt.

## The mistakes that cost the most

**Booking the freight before the shipment plan.** Fulfilment centre assignments decide how cartons are labelled and where the container goes. Plan in Send to Amazon first.

**Cartons over Amazon's limits.** An oversized or overweight box can be refused or reworked at your cost in the US, where labour costs far more than in Shenzhen. Build cartons to the limits before they are packed into a container. Our guide to [avoiding FBA prep rejections](avoid-amazon-fba-prep-rejections-china.html) lists the usual faults.

**Planning on port-to-port transit.** A ship docking in Long Beach is not stock live on Amazon. Plan to the check-in date.

**A late ISF.** It is a 24-hour rule with a penalty attached. Make sure whoever files it has the data from your supplier in time.

**An importer number or bond that is not ready.** Check both before the container sails. Our [Form 5106 guide](cbp-form-5106-importer-number-voided-2026.html) covers what to check if you have not imported for a while.

**Missing the LCL cut-off before the holiday.** Consolidation warehouses close their books earlier than full-container bookings, so shared-container cargo has the earliest deadline of all.
