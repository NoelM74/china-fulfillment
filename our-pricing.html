Good to Know

What the price includes, and what it doesn't

Included at no charge

  • Receiving from any number of suppliers
  • Standard visual inspection of every batch
  • Standard mailers and small boxes for every order
  • Packing in your own branded materials
  • Store and marketplace integration, with tracking uploaded automatically
  • Insurance claims filed for you if a shipment is lost or damaged in transit

Not in the fulfilment price

  • Shipping, quoted per shipment
  • Import duty, VAT and GST. On DDP shipments these are paid within the shipping price, but the importer's legal obligations stay with you. See who is the importer of record.
  • Product certificates and registrations (for example FDA, CPC or CE), which belong to the seller or manufacturer
  • Printing custom packaging, quoted per run

Pricing Questions

Common questions about our prices

If yours isn't here, ask us. We answer every enquiry within 24 hours.

How does the 30 days of free storage work?
It applies to new customers who start shipping. The 30 days start when your first goods arrive at our Shenzhen warehouse and run for 30 calendar days, as long as your first order or shipment leaves within that time. After that, storage is $0.49 per CBM per day. If nothing ships in the first 30 days, the standard rate applies from the day your goods arrived. There is no credit card to give and no minimum volume.
Is packaging included in the $0.99?
Yes. Standard packaging, meaning mailers and small boxes, is included in the $0.99 pick and pack. If you supply your own branded boxes, mailers, tissue or inserts, we pack with those at no extra charge. Printing custom packaging is quoted per run, from 50 pieces.
How is storage measured?
By volume, in cubic metres (CBM), billed on the space your goods actually occupy. One CBM is a 1m × 1m × 1m space. At $0.49 per CBM per day, one CBM stored for a 30-day month costs $14.70.
Do you charge receiving fees?
No. Receiving is free, whether one supplier delivers or ten, and however often they deliver. Every delivery is counted against your purchase order on arrival.
Is there a minimum volume, setup fee or contract?
No. There are no setup fees, no account fees, no minimum volumes, no lock-in contracts and no cancellation fees. You pay for what you use.
Why is shipping not on the price list?
Because it depends on each parcel's weight and size, the destination, and the service you choose: express courier, air freight, sea freight or a DDP line. We quote shipping per shipment so the price matches the real parcel. Our shipping times and costs guide shows typical ranges.
Who pays import duty and VAT?
The seller, or the importer the seller appoints. On DDP shipments the duty is paid within the shipping price, so your customer pays nothing at the door, but the legal obligations as importer stay with you. Our importer of record guide explains how this works in the US, EU and UK.
